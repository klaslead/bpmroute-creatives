# Happy Diwali 2026 reel – BPMroute

Vertical reel, 1080x1920, 20 s, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC. Soundtrack is original (synthesised in `music.py`, 120 BPM), so it is safe to post with audio.

Story: a BPMroute pulse line ignites a diya → a ring of diyas lights up on each sitar pluck while a radial EQ dances to the music → "Lights. Sweets. Family. Bass. Turn it all up." → zoom into the flame and drop: "शुभ दीपावली · Happy Diwali." with fireworks and sky lanterns → liquid-glass "Now playing: Happy Diwali" player → a firework that bursts into the BPMroute pulse logo → logo outro, bpmroute.com, by EKSTAC33 Production.
